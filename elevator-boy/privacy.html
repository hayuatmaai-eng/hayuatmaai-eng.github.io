The Elevator Boy: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The Elevator Boy is a game by Hayuatma ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game keeps on your phone

Your best score, coins, shop items, badges, daily goals, settings and, if something goes wrong, a short error log are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Error reports

If the game hits an error, you can choose to send an error report from the title screen. It contains the game version, your device model and browser version, and what was happening in the game. Nothing is sent unless you tap the button and choose where to send it.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads (for example, a second wind). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The VIP Pass is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. We only learn that the pass was bought, so the game can unlock it. We never see your card details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
